News

Headlines from up to four RSS or Atom feeds, shown on their own page of the clock. The display has no accented letters, so they are shown without their accents.

News page
When off, nothing is fetched and paging skips it.
Feeds

Paste the address of the feed itself (it usually ends in /rss, /feed or .xml), not of the website. Leave the name empty to use the feed's own title. An empty address turns that slot off.

Where a site serves its feed on plain http:// as well, use that: an https:// connection takes a large share of the clock's memory.
