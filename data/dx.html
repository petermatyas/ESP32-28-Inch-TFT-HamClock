DX Cluster

The clock holds a telnet session to a public cluster node and shows the spots it sends. The same filters apply on the display, where they can also be changed with the FILTER button.

Connection

A cluster login identifies you on a shared amateur network, so it has to be your own callsign — there is no default and nothing connects until you enter one. Node operators log every connection.

Known open nodes: hrd.wa9pie.net:8000, dxfun.com:8000, dxc.nc7j.com:7373.
